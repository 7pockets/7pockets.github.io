PokerNow Release Notes

Version 0.1 - Initial Release

We're excited to announce the launch of PokerNow, your new favorite companion for live poker games! This initial release brings the thrill of poker night to your mobile device with a range of features designed to enhance your gameplay experience.

New Features:

  • Game Creation: Easily set up new poker games with a unique game ID.
  • Join Existing Games: Use a game ID to join games created by friends.
  • Real-time Multiplayer: Enjoy seamless, lag-free gameplay with our WebSocket integration.
  • Dynamic Game Phases: Experience all phases of a poker game, from pre-flop to showdown.
  • Automatic Chip Tracking: Say goodbye to manual chip counting - we handle it all for you!
  • Hand Rankings: Built-in support for standard poker hand rankings.
  • Player Actions: Perform all standard poker actions including check, call, raise, and fold.
  • Lobby System: Wait for all players to join before the game begins.
  • Responsive Design: Enjoy a smooth experience on various screen sizes.

Technical Highlights:

  • Secure WebSocket connections for real-time updates
  • Efficient state management for a responsive UI
  • Robust error handling for a stable gaming experience

Known Issues:

  • In rare cases, players may need to refresh their app if they encounter synchronization issues.
  • The chat feature is still in development and will be available in a future update.

We're committed to continually improving PokerNow. Your feedback is crucial in helping us enhance the app. Please don't hesitate to reach out with any suggestions or issues you encounter.

Enjoy your game, and may the best hand win!
