Project River

Working title In development

Offsuit Labs is rebuilding PokerNow from scratch. Project River is a codename: the new app gets its real name when it launches.

  • Same idea: one table, everyone on their own phone, and nobody shuffling or counting chips.
  • Built from scratch: with priorities set by your answers to the 0.8.0 survey.
  • Want to know when it's ready? Join the list.

Version 0.8.0

Transition Release Coming soon

A short bridge release while we rebuild. It tells everyone what's happening and asks what you'd like from the new app.

What's in it

  • ChangedRebuild notice: the app now opens with a screen explaining that PokerNow is being rebuilt and that the game servers are offline.
  • NewSurvey: a short, optional survey about how you play and what you want from the new app.
  • NewSeven languages: English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, Arabic, Russian and Chinese.
  • ChangedAndroid 16 ready: updated for the latest Android version, and much smaller to download.
  • PausedOnline games: you can't create or join tables while the servers are offline.

Version 0.1

Initial Beta Release

The first beta, released on Google Play as PokerNow: Live Table Poker. It brings poker night to your phone: cards are dealt digitally so nobody can mark them, and every chip is counted automatically. Gather your friends, open the app and deal.

New features

  • NewGame creation: set up a new table in seconds and get a unique game ID.
  • NewJoin by game ID: type in a friend's game ID to take a seat at their table.
  • NewLobby: wait until everyone has joined before the first hand is dealt.
  • NewReal-time multiplayer: every action appears on every device as soon as it happens.
  • NewFull hand flow: every phase of a hand, from pre-flop through flop, turn and river to showdown.
  • NewPlayer actions: check, call, raise and fold.
  • NewAutomatic chip tracking: no more counting chips by hand. Bets and pots are tallied for you.
  • NewHand rankings: standard poker hand rankings decide every showdown.
  • NewResponsive design: works well on phones and tablets of any size.

Under the hood

  • TechEncrypted, secure WebSocket connections for real-time updates
  • TechEfficient state management keeps the UI fast
  • TechRobust error handling for stable sessions

Known issues

  • KnownOccasionally a device gets out of sync with the table. Restarting the app fixes it.
  • KnownTable chat isn't available yet. It's coming in a future update.
