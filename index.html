Built for the table, not the casino

nFold is for people playing together in the same room. The app does the dealing and the bookkeeping so you can concentrate on the game.

♠

Unmarkable cards

Every hand is dealt digitally, so there are no worn decks, no bent corners and no peeking.

♥

Automatic chip tracking

Every bet, call and raise is tallied for you and the pot is always right. You never have to count a stack again.

♦

Real-time multiplayer

Every action shows up on every phone at the table as soon as it happens.

♣

Every phase of the hand

Pre-flop, flop, turn, river and showdown, with the standard actions: check, call, raise and fold.

♛

Hand rankings built in

Standard poker hand rankings decide the showdown, so there's nothing to argue about at the end of a hand.

★

No account needed

Pick a name and start playing. No email or password, and no sign-up form.

Deal in under a minute

Three steps from opening the app to the first hand.

Create a table

One player starts a new game and gets a unique game ID.

Share the ID

Friends type in the ID to join. They wait in the lobby until everyone has arrived.

Shuffle up & deal

Start the game and play. nFold takes care of the cards, the chips and the pot.
